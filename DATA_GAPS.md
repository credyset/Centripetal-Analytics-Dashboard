For commercial work, maintain actual held date, qualification decision and original opportunity acceptance/win dates with a next step. Keep source collection, source event dates and integration/publication time separate. Do not fill missing dates with zeros, add overlapping Home/CSV/person inventories, treat rolling creator-window changes as pure growth, or convert source status changes into commercial decisions.
